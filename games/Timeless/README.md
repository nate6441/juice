# Timeless

**Team Members:** Helen Gelagay, Yohannes Biruk, Yididya Wondimagegnehu, Nathnael Mengistu, Leul Adane

**How would you pitch your game in one line?**
It's like Assassin's Creed meets Myst, but with the ability to travel through time by touching artifacts in a museum to solve historical mysteries and earn points. with time-altering puzzles and discovering events in the past. 


**What is the key mechanic(s) that make it fun?**
Timeless Interaction: Manipulate past events affecting the present.
Non-Verbal Storytelling: The story is conveyed entirelt through visuals, body language and sound.


**How does the game actually play? (Core Game Loop)**
Touch an artifact in a museum 
Interact with past historical events 
Solve time-based puzzles 
Make choices that alter the world 
Unlock new abilities to progress


**How will you keep players engaged for 30 minutes? What's the general scope of your game (will change as you go)?**
Emotional Engagement: Players will be drawn into the emotional narrative as they explor past historical events. The languageless storytelling will encourage them to pay close attention to visual and environmental clues, creating a sense of curiosity.
Solving Problems: The different puzzles are designed to be engaging and varied, where each sloved puzzle brings immediate change.
Multiple and Length: The game's core mission will be designed to last about 30 minutes, with each mission unlocking a new area of exploration,new puzzles and new histories to uncover. As the player progresses, the story builds up to a final emotional depth and reveal more about the past histories.
