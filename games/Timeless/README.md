## Pitch

It's like *Assassin's Creed* meets *Myst*, but with the ability to travel through time by touching artifacts in a museum to solve historical mysteries and earn points—with time-altering puzzles and discovering events in the past.

## Key Mechanics

- **Timeless Interaction:** Manipulate past events to affect the present.
- **Non-Verbal Storytelling:** The story is conveyed entirely through visuals, body language, and sound.

## Core Game Loop

1. Touch an artifact in a museum.
2. Interact with past historical events.
3. Solve time-based puzzles.
4. Make choices that alter the world.
5. Unlock new abilities to progress.

## Player Engagement & Scope

- **Emotional Engagement:** Players will be drawn into the emotional narrative as they explore past historical events. The languageless storytelling will encourage them to pay close attention to visual and environmental clues, creating a sense of curiosity.
- **Solving Problems:** The different puzzles are designed to be engaging and varied, where each solved puzzle brings immediate change.
- **Multiple and Length:** The game's core mission will be designed to last about 30 minutes, with each mission unlocking a new area of exploration, new puzzles, and new histories to uncover. As the player progresses, the story builds up to a final emotional depth and reveals more about the past histories.
